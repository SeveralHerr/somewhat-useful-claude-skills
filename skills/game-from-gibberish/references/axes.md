# Design axes

`scripts/seed.py` parses this file. Every line starting with `- ` under a `## heading`
is one draw candidate for that axis. Prose between headings is ignored, so you can
annotate freely as long as you don't use `- ` bullets for the notes.

Edit these lists. They are the actual soul of the skill — the script only rolls dice,
these lines decide what the dice can land on. The one rule when adding entries: an
entry earns its place by being hard to reach from a blank page. "collect coins" and
"shoot enemies" are what the model produces unaided; they don't need a wordlist's help.
Anything already sitting in the middle of the distribution makes the draw weaker.

## constraint

- you cannot stop moving
- every action is announced one beat before it resolves
- you can only undo, never do
- the controls are shared with an NPC who has their own plans
- one input at a time, and switching costs you a turn
- you always face the last thing you touched
- the level is also the UI, and damaging one damages the other
- you can carry exactly one verb at a time
- anything you place becomes permanent terrain
- the camera belongs to someone else and follows their interests
- you act only on the beat
- the map is drawn only where you have already been, and it is drawn wrong
- two things always move when you move one
- you may not act twice in the same place
- the game continues at the same speed while any menu is open
- your last three actions replay behind you, on a delay
- nothing can be destroyed, only relocated
- the exit moves whenever it is directly visible
